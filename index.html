About MINFO

MINFO Citation Generator is a free citation tool that helps users create accurate and properly formatted citations with ease. A Quick Cite Tool (QCT), the name “MINFO” comes from “Mi,” meaning “me,” and “Info,” which is shortened from “information,” reflecting a personal and accessible approach to managing academic sources. MINFO may also come from the Ministry of Information (MINFO), a cabinet ministry of the micronational government of the Kingdom of Arlandica, a nation-building project. The website is built entirely by students in Davao City, Philippines, guided by the principle “By Students, For Students,” solidifying its focus on supporting learners in their academic work.

The platform is designed to be simple and user-friendly, allowing students to generate citations in different formats quickly without needing advanced knowledge of citation styles. It aims to reduce the time and effort required in formatting references, helping users focus more on their research and writing.

Find Out How to Use

Step 1: Choose Your Citation Style

At the very top, click the dropdown menu under Choose Citation Style. Select the style required for your paper or project. It starts on APA 7th. The list has eight choices: APA 7th, MLA 9th, Chicago 17th (Notes & Bibliography and Author-Date), Harvard, IEEE, Vancouver and AMA 11th.

Step 2: Choose the Source Type and Fill In the Details

Pick whether your source is a Book, a Journal article, or a Website. The form shows only the boxes that source needs. Fill in what you know and leave the rest blank.

Authors
Type the surname in the first box and the given names in the second. Click "+ Add author" for more authors. For an organization, type its name in the first box and leave the second empty.

Titles
Type the title as it appears on the source. If it has a subtitle, type that in the Subtitle box; the tool joins the two with a colon. The tool then applies the capital-letter rule of the style you chose (sentence case for APA, Harvard, IEEE and Vancouver; title case for MLA and Chicago). It cannot recognise every name or place, so check those.

Step 3: Generate and Copy the Citation

Click the large blue "Process Now" button at the bottom. Your citation appears below it, with italics where the style needs them. Click "Copy citation" to paste it into your paper, then read the short "Check before you submit" notes under the result.

For librarians: Cataloging Mode

At the top of the Generator, switch from Standard Academic Mode to Cataloging Mode. Choose AACR2 or RDA, then work through the four steps: what you are cataloging, title and responsibility, edition and publication, and physical description with series and ISBN or ISSN. If the author, the year or the medium is missing, the tool asks before it builds the record. You get a readable record (AACR2 areas with ISBD punctuation, or the RDA element set) and the matching MARC 21 fields.
